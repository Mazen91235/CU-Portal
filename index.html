How Did We Start

In 2024, Mazen came up with the idea of creating a platform where university students could study, share resources, and connect. But the idea remained simple — a seed that hadn’t yet grown.

After a year - In 2025 - Mohamed came with a similar concept, but with a fresh perspective focused on what students actually need in their academic journey. When Mohamed shared his vision, Mazen saw the spark of his old idea reignited — only stronger this time. They teamed up, determined to build something real and impactful.

Shahd, a former member of the team, suggested creating a survey to gather feedback and opinions from students — a step that helped shape the direction of the platform based on real needs.

To turn their vision into action, they reached out to Jana, a skilled programmer who helped lay the technical foundation. Soon after, Yasmine, a professional full-stack developer, joined to support the development process. The UI/UX team — Marwa, Hadeer, and Somaya — played a crucial role in designing the user experience and interface, ensuring the platform was both intuitive and visually appealing.

Seven minds, one mission — to create a platform that transforms the student experience and brings the university community closer together.
